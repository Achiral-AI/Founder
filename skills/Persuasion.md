# Persuasion

Renders writing designed to change a reader's mind, create conviction, or motivate action without manipulation or hype.

## Use When

- The draft must move the reader from doubt to belief or from belief to action.
- The piece needs sharper stakes, proof, or emotional logic.
- The author wants conviction without coercion.

## Edit Moves

- Start from the reader's existing belief, not the author's desired conclusion.
- Make the cost of inaction concrete.
- Use evidence, examples, and implications in a deliberate sequence.
- Address the strongest likely objection honestly.
- Replace hype with specificity.
- Make the requested action or changed belief unmistakable.

## Questions

- What does the reader currently believe?
- What would make changing their mind feel reasonable?
- What objection deserves respect?

## Stop When

The reader can see why the conclusion follows and feels invited rather than pressured.
