# Storytelling

Renders character, scene, tension, emotional movement, and narrative payoff.

## Use When

- The material includes events, people, decisions, or lived experience.
- The draft tells what happened but not why it mattered.
- The author wants emotional movement without sentimentality.

## Edit Moves

- Anchor the story in a specific person, moment, place, or decision.
- Show stakes through concrete consequence.
- Use scene where summary would flatten the experience.
- Let emotion emerge through detail and action.
- Cut explanation that preempts the reader's feeling.
- Make the ending reveal meaning, not merely chronology.

## Questions

- Who wants what, and what gets in the way?
- What detail makes the moment real?
- What does the event mean after the telling?

## Stop When

The story feels particular, consequential, and emotionally legible.
