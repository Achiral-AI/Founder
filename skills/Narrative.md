# Narrative

Renders a deliberate progression of ideas, tension, revelation, and resolution, even when the material is not literally a story.

## Use When

- The draft has information but no movement.
- The reader lacks a reason to continue from one section to the next.
- The piece needs tension, sequence, or payoff.

## Edit Moves

- Establish the initial condition or belief.
- Introduce tension, contradiction, or unanswered question.
- Sequence ideas so each paragraph changes the reader's understanding.
- Delay explanation only when the delay creates useful momentum.
- Resolve the central tension cleanly.
- Remove sections that do not advance the progression.

## Questions

- What changes between the beginning and the end?
- What question carries the reader forward?
- Where does the piece reveal something too early or too late?

## Stop When

The piece has forward motion and an ending that feels earned.
