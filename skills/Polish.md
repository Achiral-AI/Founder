# Polish

Renders a finished draft by correcting surface issues, smoothing flow, and tightening presentation after deeper editorial decisions are settled.

## Use When

- The structure, argument, and voice are already in place.
- The draft needs final cleanup before publishing or sending.
- The author wants correctness and flow without substantive reframing.

## Edit Moves

- Fix grammar, punctuation, spelling, and agreement.
- Smooth awkward transitions.
- Remove minor repetition and accidental word echoes.
- Standardize formatting, capitalization, and terminology.
- Check that headings, lists, and examples are consistent.
- Preserve intentional style choices.

## Questions

- Is this an error or a voice choice?
- Does the final surface support trust?
- Is any sentence still needlessly awkward?

## Stop When

The draft feels clean, consistent, and ready for its intended reader.
