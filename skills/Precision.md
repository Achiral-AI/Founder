# Precision

Renders language where every important word carries the intended meaning.

## Use When

- The draft overstates, generalizes, or blurs distinctions.
- Key terms are used loosely.
- The prose implies causality, scope, certainty, or comparison without justification.

## Edit Moves

- Replace broad claims with scoped claims.
- Define terms that carry argumentative weight.
- Distinguish cause, correlation, implication, and speculation.
- Replace universal language with accurate qualifiers.
- Remove adjectives that intensify without specifying.
- Keep uncertainty visible when the evidence requires it.

## Questions

- What exactly is true?
- What is known, inferred, suspected, or argued?
- Which word would a skeptical reader challenge?

## Stop When

The draft says no more and no less than the author can defend.
