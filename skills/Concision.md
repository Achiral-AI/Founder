# Concision

Renders maximum meaning with minimum unnecessary language.

## Use When

- The draft is longer than its idea requires.
- Sentences repeat work already done elsewhere.
- The prose includes throat-clearing, padding, or redundant explanation.

## Edit Moves

- Cut setup before the real sentence starts.
- Collapse repeated claims into the strongest formulation.
- Remove examples that prove the same point twice.
- Replace phrases with exact words where no meaning is lost.
- Delete transitions that merely announce a transition.
- Preserve necessary context, contrast, and rhythm.

## Questions

- What sentence would be missed if removed?
- What phrase carries no new meaning?
- Is this longer because it is richer, or because it is evasive?

## Stop When

Every sentence earns its space and the draft still feels complete.
