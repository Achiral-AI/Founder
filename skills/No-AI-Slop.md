# No-AI-Slop

Removes writing patterns that make a draft feel generic, over-smoothed, or AI-produced while preserving the author's intended meaning.

## Use When

- The prose sounds competent but interchangeable.
- The draft leans on filler, obvious transitions, inflated balance, or generic insight.
- The author wants sharper native-English phrasing without losing their voice.

## Edit Moves

- Replace vague setup phrases with the actual claim.
- Remove hedging that exists only to sound reasonable.
- Cut generic contrast structures such as "not just X, but Y" unless the contrast earns its place.
- Prefer concrete nouns and active verbs over abstract packaging.
- Replace familiar internet phrasing with language specific to the argument.
- Keep useful roughness when it carries personality, conviction, or rhythm.

## Avoid

- Polishing everything into the same smooth register.
- Adding enthusiasm, hype, or moral grandstanding.
- Rewriting the author's point into a safer or more conventional one.

## Stop When

The draft reads like a specific person making a specific point, not like a model producing a generally acceptable answer.
