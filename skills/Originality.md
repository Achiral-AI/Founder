# Originality

Renders distinctive ideas, metaphors, formulations, and angles rather than familiar internet language.

## Use When

- The draft says true things in expected ways.
- The author wants a more differentiated angle.
- The piece relies on inherited phrases, obvious metaphors, or common frames.

## Edit Moves

- Find the least generic version of the claim.
- Replace borrowed frameworks with the author's own distinctions.
- Use metaphors that reveal structure, not decoration.
- Seek a fresher angle before polishing sentences.
- Remove language that could appear unchanged in many similar posts.
- Preserve clarity; novelty that obscures meaning is not useful.

## Questions

- What is the familiar version of this point?
- What has the author noticed that others usually miss?
- Which phrase feels found rather than inherited?

## Stop When

The work feels recognizably differentiated without trying too hard.
