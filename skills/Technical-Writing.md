# Technical-Writing

Renders accuracy, explicit terminology, procedural clarity, and reproducibility.

## Use When

- The draft explains systems, methods, APIs, research, engineering, or operations.
- Readers need to reproduce a process or understand exact behavior.
- Ambiguous terminology could cause mistakes.

## Edit Moves

- Define terms before relying on them.
- Separate concept, procedure, example, and caveat.
- State prerequisites, inputs, outputs, and expected results.
- Use ordered steps only when sequence matters.
- Replace vague behavior descriptions with observable behavior.
- Preserve technical accuracy over elegance.

## Questions

- What would a reader need to reproduce this?
- Which term could mean different things to different readers?
- What failure mode or edge case matters?

## Stop When

The writing is accurate, usable, and difficult to misapply.
