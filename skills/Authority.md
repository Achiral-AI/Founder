# Authority

Renders confident, credible writing without fake certainty, credential signaling, or inflated language.

## Use When

- The draft sounds tentative despite having a strong point.
- The prose tries to sound important instead of being useful.
- The author needs credibility without posturing.

## Edit Moves

- Replace defensive caveats with precise scope.
- State conclusions directly when they are earned.
- Use plain verbs instead of prestige vocabulary.
- Remove self-congratulation and credential padding.
- Acknowledge uncertainty once, cleanly, where it matters.
- Prefer grounded confidence over volume.

## Questions

- What can the author say plainly?
- Where is the prose asking status language to do evidence's job?
- What qualification increases trust rather than weakens it?

## Stop When

The writing feels calm, competent, and earned.
