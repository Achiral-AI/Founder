# Voice

Preserves and strengthens the author's recognizable linguistic personality.

## Use When

- A draft has become generically polished.
- Multiple editorial passes have flattened the author's style.
- The user values personality, rhythm, and recognizable phrasing.

## Edit Moves

- Preserve characteristic sentence rhythm where it helps meaning.
- Keep idiosyncratic but clear turns of phrase.
- Restore the author's preferred level of directness, warmth, humor, or restraint.
- Remove imported phrasing that does not sound like the author.
- Strengthen recurring verbal habits only when they serve the piece.
- Prefer authenticity over generic elegance.

## Questions

- What sounds distinctly like this author?
- What was lost in the polish?
- Which imperfections create trust or presence?

## Stop When

The draft is clearer and stronger while still sounding unmistakably authored.
