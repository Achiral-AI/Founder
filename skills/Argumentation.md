# Argumentation

Renders a coherent progression from premise to reasoning to evidence to conclusion.

## Use When

- The draft argues for an interpretation, recommendation, or belief.
- Claims appear in a loose order.
- The piece needs stronger logical progression.

## Edit Moves

- State the central claim clearly.
- Identify premises and put them before dependent conclusions.
- Connect evidence to the claim it supports.
- Make reasoning explicit where readers might not infer it.
- Address counterarguments in the strongest fair form.
- End with the conclusion the argument has earned.

## Questions

- What is the argument, not just the topic?
- Which premise must be accepted first?
- Does the evidence support this conclusion or a narrower one?

## Stop When

The reader can trace the logic without guessing how the parts connect.
