# Brand-Writing

Renders consistent positioning, vocabulary, personality, and brand identity.

## Use When

- The draft represents a company, product, or public-facing brand.
- The writing must align with established positioning.
- The piece needs consistency across tone, claims, vocabulary, and audience promise.

## Edit Moves

- Anchor the copy in the brand's real positioning.
- Use vocabulary the brand can own and repeat.
- Align tone with the buyer's situation and the product's seriousness.
- Replace generic benefit language with specific value.
- Keep claims consistent with what the product actually does.
- Remove personality that entertains but weakens trust or fit.

## Questions

- What does this brand believe, reject, or make easier?
- Which words should become recognizable brand assets?
- What promise is being made, explicitly or implicitly?

## Stop When

The writing feels like it belongs to the same company every time and advances the brand's position.
